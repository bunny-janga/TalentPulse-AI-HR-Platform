TalentPulse AI — Terms of Service

1. Authorized Enterprise Use
TalentPulse AI is an enterprise workforce intelligence platform intended solely for authorized personnel (System Administrators, People Operations/HR teams, and verified active employees). Unauthorized access attempts are monitored and recorded.
2. Machine Learning Predictive Ethics
Flight risk scores and predictive retention blueprints generated by TalentPulse are decision-support heuristics designed to identify burnout and retention opportunities. They must not be utilized as the sole deterministic criteria for disciplinary actions or termination.
3. Employee Feedback Confidentiality
Submissions made through the Employee Sentiment & Well-Being Pulse are handled under strict confidentiality protocols. Data is securely cataloged within the organizational SQLite data store (`talentpulse.db`) and accessible only by designated People Operations leads.
4. Data Security & Cryptographic Integrity
User passwords are encrypted with one-way PBKDF2 cryptographic hashing. Token-based registration invites and password recovery links expire upon utilization.
5. Compliance & Regulatory Alignment
Organizations operating TalentPulse agree to comply with applicable workforce privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and EEOC workplace fairness guidelines).

Workforce AI Privacy Policy

Data Collection Scope
We collect employee tenure, average monthly working hours, job satisfaction ratings, performance evaluation benchmarks, and department classifications for internal analytical evaluation.
AI Processing Transparency
Our retention engine uses calibrated predictive models and Generative AI reasoning to suggest constructive retention interventions. Models are designed to minimize algorithmic bias and prioritize sustainable workload balancing.
Zero External Data Selling
Enterprise employee data remains entirely self-contained within your local instance and is never sold, leased, or transmitted to third-party advertising brokers.
